How teachers can use this view

Look for combinations, not labelsUse attendance, GPA and study-time patterns together to decide which students may benefit from a supportive check-in.
Use tutoring and support as contextCompare groups to explore where additional academic support may be useful. Differences do not prove that a program caused an outcome.
Keep student context in viewAbsences can reflect health, family, transport or other circumstances. Treat indicators as prompts for conversation, not judgments.
